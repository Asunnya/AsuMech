------------------------------------------------------------------------
PF vocabulary
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Toxic Friends: the phase 1 raidplan.
  - Roles Mortal Slayer: a Mortal Slayer strat with its own raidplan,
    separate from Toxic Friends.
  - Modified Bin (3VJ0): a slightly modified bin for the M12S strats;
    usually means the whole pastebin. 3VJ0 = the end of its URL.
  - Uptime: Uptime Idyllic.
  - Banana Codex: a Replication 2 strat not in the main pastebins,
    "relative D north".
  - DN Replication / DN Idyllic: the DN versions of those strats; DN
    Replication 1 uses the phase 2 markers below.
  - 3-1 Orb Soaks: (not explained in the source).
