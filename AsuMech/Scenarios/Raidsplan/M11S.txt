------------------------------------------------------------------------
PF vocabulary
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - TF: the Toxic Friends raidplan, the one PF usually means.
  - Kindred No Buddies: the Kindred Flatliner strat.
  - Toxic No Buddies: the Toxic Friends Flatliner strat.
  - No Buddies: "buddies" was an early Flatliner strat where the prey
    players paired up on tethers and mirrored their tower knockbacks.
    No Buddies means not doing that.
  - PTT / TTT / PPP ...: order of the tower soaks at the end of the
    fight, P = party, T = tank (PTT = Party, Tank, Tank).
  - Uptime ES / Fixed ES: the two strats for Ecliptic Stampede (the
    two-fold / four-fold fire mechanic); raidplans below.

Flatliner: which marker corner?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Toxic Friends and Kindred use nearly the same Flatliner strat; the
    only difference is where prey stands on a static marker.
  - Check whether your marker set puts prey on the INSIDE or the
    OUTSIDE corner of the marker, and ask your PF lead which set they
    placed.
