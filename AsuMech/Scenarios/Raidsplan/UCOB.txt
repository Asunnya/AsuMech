@strat: Tuufless (Elemental)
@layout: Elemental
@plans: -
Guide: Tuufless's FFXIV Notes - UCoB (Elemental), overview and cheat sheet
  https://ffxiv.tuufless.com/elemental/ucob/

  PF largely follows Clees' UCoB guide, with these differences:
  - Neurolinks are placed in an inverted triangle.
  - Blackfire Trio towers are flipped for tanks and healers (healers take
    the tower closer to the Megaflare stack).
  - Heavensfall Trio spread positions are different (both tanks always go
    towards Nael, both ranged always opposite Nael).
  - Tenstrike Trio is done with one safe spot (the south marker) instead
    of two.

  Roles: H1 regen healer, H2 shield healer, D1/D2 melee, D3 physical
  ranged, D4 caster.

Tuufless P1: Twintania
------------------------------------------------------------------------
@scenarios: UcobP1TwintaniaScenario
@strat: Tuufless (Elemental)
@layout: Elemental
@plans: -
Guide: Tuufless - Twintania
  https://ffxiv.tuufless.com/elemental/ucob/01_twintania/

  - Neurolinks on markers 1, 2 and 3 (inverted triangle: 1 NW, 2 NE,
    3 S). Twintania starts on the NW marker, then each push takes her
    clockwise to the next marker.
  - Twintania faces away from the party; everyone but the tanks keeps a
    clear path to her rear.
  - 1st phase Fireball: 5-man stack behind Twintania. MT, ST and D3 stay
    out (D4 joins unless D3 is the target). Shields and mitigation, no
    Reprisal/Feint/Addle (the point is to build LB).
  - Liquid Hell: D3 baits; if D3 has Hatch, H2 or D4 baits that set.
    Everyone else stays near Twintania.
  - Generate: keep clear of the line between Twintania and the
    Neurolink. Hatch targets wait beside (not in) their Neurolink, bait
    Twister outside, then step in.
  - Two Hatches: the two targets take separate Neurolinks; everyone stays
    outside the triangle of Twintania and the two Neurolinks.
  - Last phase Fireball: full party stack behind Twintania; the Liquid
    Hell target keeps its puddles off the stack.
  - Transition: stack on the south marker (tanks may go north). Spread
    between the Heavensfall cones, then rotate away from south for the
    second set. ST invulns Dalamud Dive.

Tuufless P2: Nael deus Darnus
------------------------------------------------------------------------
@scenarios: UcobP2NaelScenario
@strat: Tuufless (Elemental)
@layout: Elemental
@plans: -
Guide: Tuufless - Nael deus Darnus
  https://ffxiv.tuufless.com/elemental/ucob/02_nael/

  - MT centres Nael facing north.
  - 1st Thunder priority (first line), left to right:
      MT ST H1 H2 D1 D2 D3 D4
    The two Thunder targets separate from the party, still inside the
    Lunar Dynamo safe zone.
  - Fire: In > Out > In (except the 2nd fire) > In. The 2nd fire takes it
    solo away from the party; anyone still Icebitten joins it.
  - Thunder: away from the party and from each other.
  - Third line spread (Raven's Dive):
        MT  ST
          ▲
        D1  D2
    Ranged spread out appropriately.
  - Third line: after the 4th fire, players without Thunder dodge the
    Wings toward Nael; Thunder players go far from Nael.
  - Cauterize (fourth line): bait the dives away from the centre; spread
    near the first dive's spot for Meteor Stream, stack in the middle for
    the second part; avoid the ST.
  - Seventh Umbral Era: stack on the south Neurolink (marker 3), tank LB.

Tuufless P3: Bahamut Prime
------------------------------------------------------------------------
@scenarios: UcobP3QuickmarchScenario
@strat: Tuufless (Elemental)
@layout: Elemental
@plans: -
Guide: Tuufless - Bahamut Prime
  https://ffxiv.tuufless.com/elemental/ucob/03_bahamut/
Quickmarch Trio
  - The three dragons are north. MT group west, ST group east, from the
    dragons down: tank > healer > melee > ranged.
          MT▼ST                    (DPS)
        H1     H2   ->   (H1)MT★ST(H2)
        D1     D2                   ◎
          D3 D4                 DPS stack
  - After Megaflare Dive, move along the edge to dodge Twisters.
  - Megaflare markers (three DPS) stack south; the rest go north. Tanks
    take the Tempest Wing tethers and stand in front of the healers for
    Earth Shakers.
@scenarios: UcobP3BlackfireScenario
@strat: Tuufless (Elemental)
@layout: Elemental
@plans: -
Blackfire Trio
  - Stack on marker 4, stagger-step toward Nael baiting Liquid Hell; bait
    Bahamut's dive through the centre.
  - At the edge, tanks and healers turn left, DPS turn right.
  - Megaflare marks stack between the two towers opposite Nael; the
    others stand beside their tower, then step in on the second
    Hypernova. With Nael as north:
        MT   ▼   D1
        ST       D2
        H1(stack) D3
        H2       D4
    Tank: NW tower. Healer: SW tower. DPS: NE (D1 D2 D3 D4 in that
    priority) or SE tower.
  - Gather behind Bahamut in the centre for Gigaflare.
@scenarios: UcobP3FellruinScenario
@strat: Tuufless (Elemental)
@layout: Elemental
@plans: -
Fellruin Trio
  - Stack or spread around Nael by her line; tanks nearest the centre.
          T  H2  T
        D1   H1   D2
          D3   D4
  - Tanks take the Tempest Wing tethers. Neurolinks: MT toward Bahamut,
    ST toward Twintania, H1 (and the party) the remaining one. Spread
    around the Neurolink for Meteor Stream.
@scenarios: UcobP3HeavensfallScenario
@strat: Tuufless (Elemental)
@layout: Elemental
@plans: -
Heavensfall Trio
  - Spread: MT and ST toward Nael, H1/D1 left of the bosses, H2/D2 right,
    D3/D4 opposite Nael.
        MT ST
      H1     H2
      D1     D2
        D3 D4
  - Dodge along the edge like Quickmarch.
  - Towers, from Nael: CCW (excluding Nael) MT H1 D1 D3; CW (including
    Nael) ST H2 D2 D4. A tower on Nael is the ST's. Stand about as far in
    as the Neurolinks.
  - Spiral in, keep the Hypernovas off the centre, stack in the middle for
    Twintania's Fireball.
@scenarios: UcobP3TenstrikeScenario
@strat: Tuufless (Elemental)
@layout: Elemental
@plans: -
Tenstrike Trio
  - Lineup west to east: D3 H1 D1 MT ST D2 H2 D4.
  - 1st hatches: the targeted players, left to right, take NW, S and NE.
  - 2nd hatches: the first three non-targeted players, left to right;
    they wait beside the Neurolink and step in as the first ones run
    straight out to the edge.
  - Earth Shakers: the marked four spread along the north; the other four
    wait in a square on the south Neurolink (marker 3), then the groups
    swap.
@scenarios: UcobP3GrandOctetScenario
@strat: Tuufless (Elemental)
@layout: Elemental
@plans: -
Grand Octet
  - Bahamut on a cardinal: run anti-clockwise; on an intercardinal: run
    clockwise. Start opposite Bahamut (one spot over if Nael is there).
  - The player without a dive goes to Twintania's left (ccw) and dodges to
    her other side when marked.
  - With tank LB3: four players take the towers, ignore Megaflare.
  - Without tank LB3: Megaflare players stack on Twintania's right,
    non-Megaflare players take towers.
        (Divebomb) ▼ (Megaflare)
