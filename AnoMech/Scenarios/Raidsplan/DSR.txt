P5: Dark King Thordan
------------------------------------------------------------------------
@scenarios: DsrP5WrathScenario
Wrath of the Heavens - Toolbox
  https://ff14.toolboxgaming.space/?id=514795714993271&preview=1

  - Knights on the outside: relative north.
  - Blue defamation: 4 notches NW, or the line below the marker.
  - Divebomb: opposite the WAR (behind the mage).
  - Lightnings: towards the wall; party in front of the WAR.

@scenarios: DsrP5DeathScenario
Death of the Heavens - Toolbox
  https://ff14.toolboxgaming.space/?id=414790163993271&preview=1

  - WAR: relative north.
  - Pre-quake positions depend on your spot in the conga line
    (numbers 1-4 from west to east, left to right):
      Dooms 1 and 4:   E/W middle ring
      Dooms 2 and 3:   NW/NE outer wall
      Nothing 1 and 4: E/W outer wall
      Nothing 2 and 3: SW/SE outer wall
    The Nothing players "wrap" around the south half of the arena,
    the Doom players around the north half.
  - Knockback positions: Dooms anchor, non-Dooms adjust.
  - (Source page has a "DotH Quake Dodge" image - not captured here.)
