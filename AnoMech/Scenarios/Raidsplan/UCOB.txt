P1: Twintania
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Raidplan: Twintania
  https://raidplan.io/plan/9WEG4AEGVdru3hyF
Guide: Stop baiting Liquid Hells on the party!!
  https://raidplan.io/plan/19yavfslOUU0l1Gp

  Prepull setup:
      L1 | R1   tanks
      L2 | R2   healers
      L3 | R3   melee
      L4 | R4   ranged

  - Neurolink placement: normal triangle N, SW, SE (CCW, on the 1/2/3
    markers).
  - Both tanks and 1 healer stay out of the first stack for the LB
    cheese. Discuss which healer stays out.
  - Liquid Hell baits: phys ranged always baits. Caster takes the hatch
    if phys ranged gets the first hatch.

P5: Golden Bahamut
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Raidplan: Golden Bahamut
  https://raidplan.io/plan/Y4WRFLhdHSQ7oLN_
Video: how to mid-dodge / uptime-dodge exaflares
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbrTfoIxRdE

  - Akh Morn order: share > MT invuln > OT invuln > share.
  - Shared busters: kitchen-sinked with every available mitigation.
  - Mitigation plan:
      60s/90s CDs: Morn Afah 1/3/5 or 2/4
      2m CDs:      Morn Afah 1/4 or 2/5
    Discuss with co-tank / melee / caster which Morn Afahs you cover.
  - Tank LBs: none in the default plan unless it's an emergency
    (e.g. < 8-person stack, or invuln not up).
